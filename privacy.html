Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 2026

At Iqiniso, we value your trust and are fully committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website or purchase our footwear products.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information that you directly provide to us, including your name, shipping address, billing address, payment details, email address, and contact numbers when completing an order transaction.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your gathered information is securely handled to perform the following core business actions:

  • Processing, packing, and shipping your chosen footwear purchases.
  • Providing automated order confirmations and transactional tracking updates.
  • Preventing, identifying, and addressing fraudulent checkout activities.

3. Secure Local Data & Storage

We respect user preferences. While standard online checkout mechanics process operational payments securely, your digital shopping actions (like saving items to your wishlist or populating a cart) are tracked safely directly on your machine through secure localized browser variables.

4. Your Rights & Control

You have the absolute right to view, modify, or permanently request the extraction or deletion of any stored address details or account profiles. For inquiries, reach out to our dedicated support workspace.
